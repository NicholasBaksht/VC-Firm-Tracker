Jenny Lee

Senior Managing Partner (now Granite Asia) at GGV Capital · Joined 2005

View Live Interactive Profile →

Biography

Jenny Lee is the single most important person for understanding GGV Capital's Asia strategy. After early career roles at Morgan Stanley and JAFCO Asia, she joined GGV's senior leadership in 2005 and established its Shanghai office, helping turn a transpacific venture firm into one of the few franchises with durable credibility across Southeast Asia, China, and India. Her record includes Grab and other APAC champions, and she now anchors Granite Asia's post-split identity as the successor to GGV's Asia business.
